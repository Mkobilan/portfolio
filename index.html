What I've shipped

HubPlate

Point of sale and management software for restaurants

The tool I wished I'd had on every shift. One system for the front of house, the kitchen and the office.

  • Ordering, floor plans, reservations, waitlist, staff scheduling, inventory and recipe costing, loyalty and gift cards
  • An Android POS app that takes card payments through Stripe card readers and prints to the kitchen
  • A Claude connector so an owner can ask for last night's sales or build next week's schedule in plain English

Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Stripe Terminal, Capacitor, Anthropic and Gemini APIs

130K+lines of TypeScript
250+database migrations
In personI sell it door to door to San Antonio restaurants

Lead Gen Hub

Lead generation, CRM and cold email for local businesses

Finds businesses, enriches their contacts, and runs email campaigns with warm-up, reply tracking and unsubscribe handling built in. I run all of my own sales outreach through it every weekday.

Playwright scraping, IMAP and SMTP, partner API, Claude connector

Baybolt

Software for auto repair shops

Bays, mechanics, digital vehicle inspections, quotes and invoices in one place, with Stripe billing and payments for shops and their customers.

Next.js, Supabase, Stripe Connect, React Email, PDF invoices

VERDICT 2087

A 3D arena brawler in the browser, in development. Private for now; demo on request.

An innocent man fights for his freedom on a TV show run by the corporation that framed him. Ten arenas, ten bosses, a ratings meter that rewards variety, and a story told between rounds. I started it in Unity, then rebuilt it in the browser to move faster, with a desktop build ready for Steam.

Three.js, Vite, Electron, synthesized audio, Mixamo animation. Earlier prototype in Unity and C#.

How I work

I build with AI coding agents, mainly Claude Code, as my everyday tool. I decide what gets built, how it should behave and whether it's right; the agents help me write it fast. That's how one person ships seven products.

Because I sell what I build, I hear from customers directly, and I can turn what they tell me into a working change the same week.

  • ProductNext.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Android apps with Capacitor
  • Data and backendSupabase, Postgres, row-level security, APIs, webhooks, scheduled jobs
  • PaymentsStripe subscriptions, Connect and card readers
  • AI and automationClaude and Gemini APIs, MCP connectors, AI agents for outreach and content
